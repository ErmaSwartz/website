Who I Am

I care about cities because I care about people — and the systems we build shape who gets to thrive in them.

I work where data meets design and policy meets place. Over the past two years, I've built predictive models for transit equity, mapped urban waste systems, 3D-printed a wearable air quality sensor, and written a thesis on circular economy in construction — all in Python, all grounded in spatial evidence, all aimed at making cities more just.
